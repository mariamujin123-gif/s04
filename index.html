Who is George Orwell?

George Orwell was a famous British writer. His real name was Eric Arthur Blair. He was born in India and died in 1950 in London.

style He wrote many books about politics, freedom, and society.

His most famous books are Animal Farm and 1984.

Animal Farm was published in 1945 and uses animals to talk about power and unfairness.

George Orwell is still one of the most famous writers in the world.

His most important works

  1. Animal Farm (1945)
  2. 1949
  3. Nineteen Eighty-four(1984)

The Three most important topics

  • Power and control
  • Freedom
  • Political oppression
